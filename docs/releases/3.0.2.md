### Upgrade

Update to **3.0.2** through HACS or Marketplace and restart Home Assistant to load the bundled brand assets. Existing Gecko configuration and entity identities are preserved. For a manual installation, replace `custom_components/gecko` with the complete directory or extract `gecko.zip` into that directory.

CI gates publication on the integration/client tests and coverage guards, Ruff, HACS, hassfest and standalone wheel/source-distribution verification. Physical spa controls were not exercised for this branding patch.
